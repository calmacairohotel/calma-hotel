Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I book?

Choose your dates and guests, pick a room and send your details. You get a booking code instantly and our team confirms on WhatsApp.

What is included in the price?

Every room includes breakfast, air conditioning, hot water, a TV, free Wi-Fi, housekeeping, laundry service and our 24-hour reception.

Do the rooms have a view?

Our single, double and triple rooms all face the street, with either a window or a balcony.

Do I pay online?

No card is needed to send a booking request. Our team confirms availability and payment details with you directly.

Why book direct instead of Booking.com?

Same rooms and the same service, without the platform commission — so you pay less when you book on this website.

Can you arrange an airport pickup?

Yes. Add it in the notes when you book, or message us on WhatsApp with your flight details.

Is there parking?

There is no parking at the hotel itself, but there is public parking close by — ask reception and we will point you to it.

Do you organise trips?

Yes — airport transfers and day trips to the Pyramids, Old Cairo, the Egyptian Museum and the Nile. Ask us on WhatsApp or at reception.
