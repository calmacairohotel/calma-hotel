Welcome to CALMA

A house built
around stillness.

“We didn't set out to build a hotel that impresses at first glance. We built one you exhale in.”

Tucked into the rhythm of Cairo, CALMA pairs warm brass and honey-toned wood with soft linens and quiet corners — a small hotel with a deliberate, unhurried point of view. Every room is dressed the same way a good evening is: simply, and with care.
