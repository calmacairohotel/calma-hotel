Arrival program · On arrival

The Calm Welcome

We track your flight, arrange a private pickup from Cairo International Airport, and have your room ready — followed by a short orientation of the neighbourhood.

Full day · Approx. 8 hours

Pyramids & Saqqara

The Giza Plateau and the quieter Step Pyramid at Saqqara with a private, licensed guide and a driver who paces the day sensibly. Lunch at a local restaurant along the way.

A walking route through Islamic Cairo's minarets and stone alleys, ending at Khan El Khalili for tea and a slow browse through the market.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I book?

Choose your dates and guests, pick a room and send your details. You get a booking code instantly and our team confirms on WhatsApp.

What is included in the price?

Every room includes breakfast, air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, housekeeping, laundry service and our 24-hour reception.

Do the rooms have a view?

Our single, double and triple rooms all face the street, with either a window or a balcony.

Do I pay online?

No card is needed to send a booking request. Our team confirms availability and payment details with you directly.

Why book direct instead of Booking.com?

Same rooms and the same service, without the platform commission — so you pay less when you book on this website.

Can you arrange an airport pickup?

Yes. Add it in the notes when you book, or message us on WhatsApp with your flight details.
