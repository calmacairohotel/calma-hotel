The Rooms

Four ways to rest.

Each room is dressed the same way — with warm wood, soft brass, and linens that don't try too hard. Choose the layout that fits how you travel.

01Classic

Classic Twin Room

Two single beds beneath tall shuttered windows, framed art and a warm wood headboard wall. Our most requested room for friends travelling together, and for guests who simply like a little more space either side of the bed.

  • Two single beds
  • Private bathroom
  • Air conditioning
  • Smart TV
  • City-facing window

Deluxe Double Room

A generous double bed, a proper desk for slow mornings with a laptop, and deeper, softer furnishings throughout. Built for couples and for guests who want a little more room to settle in.

  • One double bed
  • Dedicated work desk
  • Private bathroom
  • Blackout curtains
  • In-room refreshments

03Family

Family Room

Multiple beds set within one warm, wood-panelled room — comfortable enough for a family or a small group to share without feeling crowded. A quiet reading corner and full-length curtains keep the room restful even in daylight.

  • Multiple beds
  • Extra storage & wardrobe
  • Private bathroom
  • Smart TV
  • Air conditioning

04Suite

Modern Suite

Our most contemporary stay — clean lines, brushed wood, a glass rain shower and low, warm lighting throughout. For guests who want CALMA's calm in a sharper, more minimal setting.

  • Glass rain shower
