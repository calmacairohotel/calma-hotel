Journeys & Programs

Cairo, at an unhurried pace.

A small set of programs we plan and arrange for our guests — sensibly paced, well guided, and built around your stay rather than a fixed script. Get in touch and we'll tailor the details.

Our Journeys & Programs

01Arrival Program

The Calm Welcome

Landing in a new city is rarely calm — this program is built to fix that. We track your flight, arrange a private pickup from Cairo International Airport, and have your room ready with a light breakfast waiting, followed by a short orientation to the neighbourhood so you know exactly where to walk on your first evening.

On LandingPrivate driver meets you at arrivals with a name board
On ArrivalEarly check-in where possible, welcome breakfast or refreshments
First EveningA short walking orientation of the streets around the hotel
  • Airport pickup
  • Welcome breakfast
  • Neighbourhood orientation
Included with select stays

02Full-Day Journey

Pyramids & Saqqara

A full day out to the Giza Plateau and the older, quieter step pyramid at Saqqara, with a private, licensed guide and a driver who paces the day sensibly rather than rushing between stops. Lunch is at a local restaurant along the way, chosen for good food over spectacle.

MorningGiza Plateau — the three pyramids and the Sphinx
MiddayLunch at a local restaurant near the plateau
AfternoonSaqqara's Step Pyramid and the surrounding necropolis
  • Private licensed guide
  • Air-conditioned transport
  • Lunch included
  • Entry tickets arranged
Approx. 8 Hours

03Half-Day Journey

Old Cairo on Foot

A walking route through Islamic Cairo's minarets and stone alleyways, ending at Khan El Khalili for tea and a slow browse through the market. Kept deliberately light on pace — this is a morning for looking up, not for checking things off a list.

MorningWalking route through historic mosques and gates
Late MorningKhan El Khalili market and a tea stop
  • Private walking guide
  • Tea stop in Khan El Khalili
  • Suitable for small groups
Approx. 4 Hours
Enquire via WhatsApp
04Half-Day Journey

Egyptian Museum, Properly Explained

The Egyptian Museum holds more than a single visit can absorb — our guide narrows it down to the pieces worth your time, including the Tutankhamun collection, and explains the history in plain terms rather than dates and labels.

  • Private Egyptologist guide
  • Entry tickets arranged
  • Round-trip transport
Approx. 3 Hours
Enquire via WhatsApp
05Evening Journey

Felucca on the Nile

A quiet hour on a traditional felucca sailboat as the city's light softens over the water — no engine, no itinerary, just the Nile at the end of the day. A gentle way to close out a busier day of sightseeing.

  • Private felucca booking
  • Timed for sunset
  • Hotel pickup & drop-off
Approx. 1.5 Hours
